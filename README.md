# 👋 About Me

Hi, I'm **Heshan Pramuditha**, a passionate **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning enthusiast** currently pursuing my **BSc (Hons) in Information Technology at the University of Kelaniya**.

I am currently working as an **Innovation & R&D Intern at Dialog Axiata PLC**, where I contribute to research and development initiatives involving emerging technologies and next-generation AI solutions.

My interests span across **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Voice & Audio Processing, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Agentic AI Systems**. I enjoy exploring how intelligent systems can be leveraged to solve real-world challenges and create meaningful impact at scale.

I have hands-on experience designing and developing end-to-end AI applications, ranging from computer vision pipelines and voice intelligence systems to LLM-powered assistants and multimodal AI solutions. My work primarily involves **Python, PyTorch, LangChain, Hugging Face, OpenCV, and modern AI frameworks**.

I am particularly interested in research-driven innovation and continuously seek opportunities to expand my expertise in intelligent systems, deep learning, and multimodal AI technologies.
